📘 CO1 REFERENCE: Linear Systems, Gaussian Methods, Cramer's Rule, Determinants, Applications

Linear Systems

Augmented matrix: $[A|B]$

Outcomes: exactly one, infinitely many, or none.

Inconsistent: a row $[0\ 0\ 0 \mid c]$ with $c\neq 0$.

Homogeneous $Ax=0$: always consistent. More unknowns than equations → infinitely many solutions.

Gaussian Methods

Gaussian: reduce to REF, then back-substitute.

Gauss-Jordan: reduce to RREF (pivots = 1, zeros above AND below).

Row ops: $R_i\leftrightarrow R_j$, $kR_i$, $R_i+kR_j$.

Free variable: a column with no pivot.

Cramer's & Determinants

Cramer: $x_i = D_i/D$, needs $D=\det A\neq 0$.

$3\times3$ expansion: $a(ei-fh)-b(di-fg)+c(dh-eg)$

Triangular: det = product of diagonal.

Scalar: $|kA|=k^n|A|$

Applications: fitting curves, word problems → build a system.

📗 CO2 REFERENCE: Inverse, Tetrahedron, Area, Line, Basis

Inverse

$2\times2$: $\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}^{-1}=\frac{1}{ad-bc}\begin{bmatrix}d&-b\\-c&a\end{bmatrix}$

Adjoint: $A^{-1}=\frac{1}{|A|}\text{adj}(A)$, adj = transpose of the cofactor matrix.

Gauss-Jordan: $[A|I]\to[I|A^{-1}]$

Solve $Ax=b$: $x=A^{-1}b$

Rules: $|A^{-1}|=1/|A|$, $\ |\text{adj}A|=|A|^{n-1}$

Area, Line, Volume, Basis

Triangle area: $\frac12\left|\det\begin{bmatrix}x_1&y_1&1\\x_2&y_2&1\\x_3&y_3&1\end{bmatrix}\right|$. Det $=0$ → collinear.

Line through 2 points: $\det\begin{bmatrix}x&y&1\\x_1&y_1&1\\x_2&y_2&1\end{bmatrix}=0$

Tetrahedron: $V=\frac16|\det|$ of the 3 edge vectors from one vertex. Det $=0$ → coplanar.

Basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$: $n$ vectors with $\det\neq0$ (independent + spanning).

📙 CO3 REFERENCE: Null Space, Column Space, Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors

Null Space

Null$(A)$: all $x$ with $Ax=0$. Subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ ($n$ = columns).

Method: RREF → free variables → parametric vector form → basis vectors.

Nullity: number of free variables $=n-\text{rank}$.

Column Space

Col$(A)$: span of the columns. Subspace of $\mathbb{R}^m$ ($m$ = rows).

Basis: the pivot columns of the ORIGINAL $A$ (not the RREF).

Rank: number of pivots $=\dim\text{Col}(A)$.

Rank–Nullity: rank + nullity $=n$.

$b\in$ Col$(A)$ iff $Ax=b$ is consistent.

Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors

Definition: $Av=\lambda v$, $v\neq0$.

Characteristic eq: $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$.

Eigenvectors: solve $(A-\lambda I)v=0$.

Shortcuts: $\sum\lambda=\text{tr}(A)$, $\prod\lambda=\det A$. Triangular → diagonal entries.

Powers: $A^k$ has $\lambda^k$, $A^{-1}$ has $1/\lambda$, same eigenvectors.
